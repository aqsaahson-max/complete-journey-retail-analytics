# Team Contributions

This project was developed collaboratively by a four-member team as part of the **IT3101 – Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence** module at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT).

The implementation, technical decisions, and analytical dashboards presented in this repository reflect the collective efforts of the team.

As team leader, I was responsible for coordinating task allocation, monitoring project progress, facilitating communication, and supporting collaboration throughout the development process.

- **Power BI:** Data modelling, dashboard development, and business intelligence reporting.

I also supported teammates in addressing technical challenges, troubleshooting implementation issues, and integrating the different components into a cohesive solution.
